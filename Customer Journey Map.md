## Journey: Logging a High-Impact Incident

| Stage | Awareness | Create Incident | Set Impact / Urgency | Assign | Save | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **User action** | Receives a report of a major outage | Opens Incident > Create New | Selects Impact = 1 - High | Chooses Assignment group and Assigned To | Clicks Submit | Reviews the incident in the list |
| **Touchpoint** | Phone / email / portal | Incident form | Impact & Urgency fields | Assignment fields | Submit button | Incident list |
| **Without controls** | Details may be missed | Fields left blank | Urgency set inconsistently | Assignee forgotten | Incomplete record saved | State edited in list, rules bypassed |
| **With this solution** | - | Form loads normally | Urgency auto-set to High and locked | Assignment group becomes mandatory | Save blocked until Assigned To is filled | State edits in list are blocked |
| **Emotion** | Urgent | Neutral | Confident | Guided | Reassured | Trusting |
| **Opportunity** | - | - | Reduce manual decisions | Enforce routing data | Prevent bad data | Protect State changes |
